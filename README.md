
App-Description (Daily Planner) 
------------------------------

Purpose: 
The purpose of the Daily Planner app is to write down your goals of the day or week. For every goal you complete you get a point and for every goal you miss you get a minus point. Depending on the amount of points you get you can achieve certain achievements. 

Target audience: 
The target audience is for students. Makes it easier for them to plan out their academic studies and give them incentive to achieve those goals. 

The main function: 
The main two functions are the Goal(write down the goals) and Check functions (check the functions you have completed). 

   - Goal function:   Write down the goals you wanna do 
   - Check function: Check the goal you have completed 
   - Edit function:    Specify at what time it should be done  
   - Summary function: Get an overview of the percentage goals you have completed.
